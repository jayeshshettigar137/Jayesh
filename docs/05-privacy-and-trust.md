# 05 — Privacy (until the reveal) & Trust

## Anonymity checklist: run it before every post

- [ ] **Chess.com username not visible** anywhere: board header, game-over popup, chat, profile, rating graph page, URL bar, browser tab titles, notifications. Crop or blur.
- [ ] **Opponent usernames blurred** too (courtesy, and a common opponent can lead people back to you).
- [ ] **No game links / game IDs** shared. A Chess.com game URL points straight to your account.
- [ ] **PGNs stripped** of `[White]`, `[Black]`, `[Site]`, `[Link]` headers before sharing. Share FEN/positions instead.
- [ ] **No exact rating screenshots from the profile page.** Rebuild the rating in your own title card/graph. (Exact rating + date + time control can be matched to an account by a determined viewer. Consider rounding in Stories if it gets risky; the daily Reel number stays exact.)
- [ ] **No full name, school, workplace, city/neighbourhood, home background** (windows, street noise, local signage).
- [ ] **Trophies/certificates/newspaper clippings**: names, dates, venues blurred.
- [ ] **Media metadata stripped** (EXIF GPS on photos). Export through the editor, don't upload originals.
- [ ] **Reflections** checked: monitor, glasses, glossy trophies, phone screens.
- [ ] **Voice**: fine to use, but avoid saying names of local clubs, coaches, schools, tournaments by year.
- [ ] **Separate accounts**: brand email, brand Instagram/YouTube/X; don't follow or get followed by your personal accounts; don't log in from personal-account "suggested to contacts" flows (phone contact sync can surface the account to people you know).
- [ ] **Payment rails** (for Stage 1 products): a checkout page can show the legal name of the merchant. Use a platform that displays the brand name, or wait until after the reveal to launch paid products.

## Chess.com account

Play on your **existing** account. Don't create a fresh one to hide identity:
- A new account starts from a provisional rating, not ~1500, so the story's starting point stops being true.
- Chess.com's rules around multiple accounts are strict; check their current Terms/Fair Play policy before doing anything unusual.

Just keep the username out of every frame until the reveal. After the reveal, share the profile publicly. It's the ultimate proof.

## Fair-play proof

A faceless account climbing fast **will** get accused of cheating. Pre-empt it:
- No engine/analysis/explorer during games. Ever.
- Keep **full uncut screen recordings** of all serious games (privately stored).
- Publish some **complete unedited games** with live commentary.
- After the reveal, the public Chess.com profile + game history gives the full audit trail.

## Brand principles

1. Real results only.
2. No fake rating.
3. No fake win streaks.
4. No fake student testimonials.
5. No guarantee of 2000 in 30 days.
6. Show failures.
7. Show rating drops.
8. Show actual training.
9. Teach what was genuinely learned.
10. Build trust before aggressive monetization.

Credential wording, always: **"2× Goa State Champion (U7 & U9)"**.
