# Agentic Company PRD — RelayOS

Status: v0.1 — September 26, 2026
Target: $10,000 collected by October 20, 2026

## 1. Product vision

RelayOS is a small, agent-operated company serving home-service businesses such as HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, cleaning, and remodeling companies.

It has three coordinated revenue lines:

1. RelayFlow — vertical micro-SaaS for lead capture, quote preparation, follow-up, and reporting.
2. RelayOps — AI automation implementation agency using RelayFlow and adjacent workflows.
3. RelayLab — original faceless media, newsletter, and digital products for home-service operators.

The businesses share one audience, one CRM, one knowledge base, one analytics layer, and one content distribution system.

## 2. Business objectives

### Immediate objective

Collect $10,000 in revenue by October 20, 2026.

Target mix:

- 4 RelayOps implementations at $1,500 = $6,000.
- 20 RelayFlow paid accounts at $99 for the first month = $1,980.
- 50 RelayLab product sales at $49 = $2,450.
- Total target = $10,430.

This is a stretch target, not a forecast. Revenue must be collected, not merely promised.

### 90-day objectives

- 25–50 active SaaS accounts.
- 5–10 agency clients on implementation or maintenance plans.
- 1,000 qualified newsletter subscribers.
- 2 validated digital products.
- At least 70% of routine operating tasks handled without manual execution.

## 3. Target customer

Primary ICP: owner-operated or small home-service businesses with 2–30 employees, existing lead volume, and weak follow-up systems.

Primary pain:

- Missed calls and web leads.
- Slow quotes.
- No systematic follow-up.
- No visibility into lead-to-job conversion.
- Owners spending time on repetitive administrative work.

Buyer: owner, operations manager, or office manager.

## 4. Product scope

### RelayFlow MVP

Must have:

- Workspace and user accounts.
- Lead intake form and webhook endpoint.
- Lead status pipeline.
- AI-generated lead summary.
- Quote-request checklist.
- Configurable email follow-up sequences.
- Human approval before external messages are sent.
- Basic dashboard: leads, response time, follow-up status, booked jobs.
- Stripe subscription billing.
- Audit log for every agent action.
- Import/export of CSV data.

Should have:

- Calendar link integration.
- Gmail or Outlook integration.
- SMS integration through a compliant provider.
- Saved business knowledge base.
- Weekly owner report.

Won’t have in MVP:

- Fully autonomous outbound sales.
- Autonomous price commitments.
- Autonomous refunds or financial decisions.
- Live trading or investment advice.
- Complex multi-location permissions.

### RelayOps initial offers

1. Lead follow-up setup — $1,500 fixed price.
2. Quote and estimate workflow — $1,500 fixed price.
3. Monthly optimization and monitoring — $500/month.

Deliverable promise: one measurable workflow live within seven calendar days, subject to customer access and approval.

### RelayLab initial products

1. Home-Service Follow-Up Kit — email templates, pipeline, SOPs, and dashboard template — $49.
2. AI Office Automation Pack — prompts, workflows, intake forms, and implementation guide — $99.
3. Weekly Home-Service Growth Brief — free newsletter leading to paid products and SaaS.

Content must be original, researched, narrated, and meaningfully edited. Do not build the business on unlicensed or minimally modified clips.

## 5. Success metrics

North-star metric: collected revenue from retained customers.

Weekly scorecard:

- Qualified prospects contacted.
- Sales conversations booked.
- Proposals sent.
- Cash collected.
- Active SaaS workspaces.
- Activation rate.
- Weekly active users.
- Churn and refunds.
- Product sales.
- Email subscribers.
- Content outputs and qualified clicks.
- Agent task success rate.
- Human escalations.
- Tool/API cost per dollar of revenue.

## 6. Safety and approval rules

Human approval is mandatory for:

- Sending first-contact outreach.
- Publishing public claims or customer stories.
- Spending money or changing subscriptions.
- Creating accounts or API keys.
- Uploading customer data.
- Changing payment, refund, or pricing logic.
- Deploying destructive database changes.
- Sending legally or financially sensitive communications.

Agents may autonomously research, draft, test, classify, summarize, monitor, and prepare actions for approval.

## 7. User stories

- As an owner, I can see every new lead and its next action.
- As an office manager, I can approve a prepared follow-up message.
- As a customer, I can receive a quote request confirmation quickly.
- As a RelayOps operator, I can clone a proven workflow into a new workspace.
- As a RelayLab subscriber, I can download practical workflows and templates.
- As the CEO, I can see which business line is producing cash and stop weak experiments.

## 8. Launch gates

RelayFlow can launch when:

- A new workspace can be created.
- A lead can be received and displayed.
- An agent can prepare a summary and follow-up draft.
- The draft requires approval before sending.
- Every action is logged.
- Stripe test and production flows are verified.
- Basic backup and rollback procedures exist.

RelayOps can sell when:

- One demo workflow works end-to-end.
- A customer-facing scope and price exist.
- A delivery checklist exists.
- The first case study is anonymized if necessary.

RelayLab can publish when:

- Content is original and source-checked.
- Copyright and platform rules are reviewed.
- Every item has a clear CTA to the newsletter, product, or SaaS.
