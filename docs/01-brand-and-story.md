# 01 — Brand & Story

## The one-line story

> **"Former 2× Goa State Champion (U7 & U9). Currently ~1500. 30 days. One goal. 2000."**

The audience follows **the story**. Later they buy **the system**. After the reveal they follow **the person**.

State the credential the same way every time: **"2× Goa State Champion (U7 & U9)"** or "2× Goa State Champion as a kid (Under-7 & Under-9)". **Never** write just "Goa State Champion". Leaving out the age groups implies an open/adult title, and once someone calls that out, the trust is gone. Saying the age groups is actually the better hook anyway: *"I was a state champion before I was 10. Now I'm stuck at 1500."*

## Name evaluation

Scoring: **Longevity** = does it survive past Day 30 and past 2000? **Search** = can it be typed and found? **Story** = does it carry the hook by itself?

| Name | Longevity | Search | Story | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The 2000 Project** | ✅ High | ✅ | ✅ | Works after Day 30 ("The 2000 Project: Season 2 → 2200"). "Project" fits the experiment framing. **Recommended primary.** |
| Road to 2000 | ⚠️ Medium | ⚠️ crowded | ✅ | Widely used phrase, so hard to own in search and hashtags. Good as a **series name** inside the brand. |
| 2000 in 30 | ❌ Low | ✅ | ✅✅ | Hits hard, but expires on Day 31 and forces a rebrand if the timeline slips. Use it as a **video/series title**. |
| The 1500→2000 Project | ⚠️ | ❌ arrows can't go in handles | ✅ | Too literal; the starting number is stale by Week 2. |
| RoadTo2000 | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ✅ | Same issues as Road to 2000. |
| The Chess Comeback | ✅ | ❌ generic | ⚠️ | Strong for the reveal era, weak as a faceless handle. |
| 30 Days to 2000 | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | Same expiry problem as "2000 in 30". |

**Recommendation:** brand = **The 2000 Project**, series = **"2000 in 30"**, recurring on-screen format = **"DAY X/30"**.

## Visual identity (faceless)

Every Short/Reel opens with the **same 1.5-second title card**. This is the signature:

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│      DAY 12/30      │
│   RATING: 1712      │
│   TARGET: 2000      │
└─────────────────────┘
```

Then **cut straight to the board**. No intro, no "hey guys".

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Title card | Black background, one accent colour (e.g. electric green for up days, red for down days), one monospace/condensed font |
| Board | Pick **one** Chess.com board and piece theme and never change it. It becomes a recognition cue |
| Recurring props | Same hoodie (hands/silhouette shots), same desk, notebook, analog chess clock, trophies with identifiers blurred |
| Rating graph | Same style every time: line chart, start line at 1500, target line at 2000 |
| Captions | Burned-in, big, 2–4 words per frame |
| Voice | **Your real voice** (recommended), face hidden. Personality and authenticity carry the brand. AI voice only as a fallback, and be consistent |
| Sign-off | Same last line every video: *"Day 12. 1712. 288 to go."* |

Shot list for the faceless look: screen recording of the board · over-the-shoulder with the face out of frame · hands on the keyboard/mouse · pen on the notebook during calculation · clock close-up · silhouette against the monitor · trophies (blurred).

## Hook bank

Real numbers only. Swap in the actual figures from `python3 -m tracker report`.

**Challenge / status**
- "I'm 1500. I have 30 days to hit 2000."
- "I need 87 more points."
- "Day 28. 1972. Three games."
- "I was a state champion as a kid. Now I'm trying to reach 2000."

**Loss / drama** (these usually beat the win hooks)
- "I just lost 100 rating in one day."
- "I thought this position was winning…"
- "This one move cost me 40 rating."
- "I threw away a completely winning position."
- "I tilted. Here's what 6 losses in a row looks like."

**Educational**
- "This mistake is keeping me at 1500."
- "3 mistakes keeping 1500s stuck."
- "I stopped blundering after I started doing this before every move."

**Participation**
- "Can you find the move I missed?"
- "Comment your rating. Would you have seen this?"
- "Guess my rating after today."

**Mystery**
- "I won my first state championship before I was 10."
- "I used to be much stronger than I am today."
- "I've been hiding something from you."

Hook rules: **under 2 seconds**, a number or a tension in the first line, show the position on screen by second 2.

## Core narrative loop (every piece of content fits here)

```
CHALLENGE → TRAINING → GAMES → FAILURE → LESSON → RATING CHANGE → NEXT CHALLENGE
```

What the audience should be thinking the whole time: **"Can this person actually do it?"** Keep the doubt alive. Don't make the win feel inevitable.

---

## Mystery arc (faceless → reveal)

Reveal one layer per week. The audience should know a real person is behind the account, just not **who**.

| Week | Reveal beat | Example content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Childhood title | "I won my first state championship before I was 10." Blurred trophy close-up |
| 2 | The fall | "I used to be much stronger than I am today." The ~1700 peak, what happened |
| 3 | Tension | "I've been hiding something from you." Hint at the reveal. Hands, voice, silhouette |
| 4 | Countdown | "Tomorrow, you meet the person behind the account." |

Only move the reveal earlier if the audience demands it **and** the account is big enough to benefit. Never reveal because a single day went badly.

## The identity reveal

**Title:** *"You've Been Watching Me for 30 Days. Here's Who I Am."*

**Cold open (0:00–0:30):** black screen → chess clock ticking → the rating graph draws itself with the **real** numbers → narration: *"For the last 30 days, you've been following a stranger trying to reach 2000."*

**Reveal (0:30–1:30):** face, name, childhood photos, the Goa State Championship (U7 & U9) trophies, unblurred.

**Body:**
1. Why the comeback started (the honest version).
2. What actually happened: the graph, the worst day, the turning point.
3. The final number. **If it's 1840, the title card says 1840.** "I didn't make it, and here's what 30 days actually does to a 1500" is still a strong video. If you fake it once, the brand is finished.
4. What's next: coaching, the course, Season 2.

**Transition:** coaching + courses + personal brand (see `04-monetization-and-funnel.md`).

Have the reveal video **edited and ready before Day 30**. Leave only the final number and the ending to cut in afterwards.
