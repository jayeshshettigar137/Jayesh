# RelayOS Revenue Plan — September 26 to October 20, 2026

## Revenue target

Collected cash target: $10,000 by October 20, 2026.

Stretch mix:

- 4 RelayOps implementations × $1,500 = $6,000.
- 20 RelayFlow accounts × $99 first month = $1,980.
- 50 RelayLab products × $49 = $2,450.
- Stretch total = $10,430.

## Week 1: September 26–October 2

- Choose one initial niche: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, or cleaning.
- Build RelayFlow demo with synthetic data.
- Create one RelayOps offer and one payment link.
- Create one RelayLab product outline.
- Build a list of 150 qualified businesses.
- Write 3 case-study-style demo examples without inventing real results.

Exit gate: demo works and 50 prospects are ready for personal outreach.

## Week 2: October 3–9

- Contact 20–30 qualified prospects per day with approval.
- Run 10 discovery calls.
- Close the first implementation client.
- Finish the first digital product.
- Publish 10 original short-form content pieces and 2 long-form pieces.
- Invite prospects to a free workflow audit.

Target: $1,500–$3,000 collected.

## Week 3: October 10–16

- Deliver the first client workflow.
- Close three additional implementation clients.
- Convert repeated workflow requirements into RelayFlow features.
- Start paid SaaS onboarding for the first users.
- Launch the newsletter and product funnel.

Target: cumulative $6,000–$8,000 collected.

## October 17–20

- Collect outstanding balances.
- Close remaining SaaS trials.
- Run a product launch campaign to the newsletter and prospects.
- Publish one customer-approved case study.
- Review every agent task and remove unnecessary automation.

Target: cumulative $10,000 collected.

## Daily operating cadence

08:00 — CEO master reviews scorecard and sets priorities.

09:00 — Sales master prepares prospect and follow-up queue.

11:00 — Engineering master runs build/test tasks.

14:00 — Growth master prepares content and campaign drafts.

17:00 — Finance/risk master checks revenue, spend, failures, and approvals.

18:00 — Human reviews the approval queue and makes consequential decisions.

## Kill and pivot rules

- If 100 qualified prospects produce fewer than 10 conversations, change the offer or niche.
- If 10 trial users produce fewer than 3 active users after seven days, revise onboarding or the product.
- If a digital product gets fewer than 5 sales from the first 300 qualified visitors, revise the offer.
- If agent/tool cost exceeds 20% of collected revenue for two weeks, reduce model usage or simplify workflows.
- If content depends on reused or unlicensed media, stop and redesign it before publishing.
