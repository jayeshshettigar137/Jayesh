# Shortlist across the 10 research reports

Caveat: all 10 reports rest on search-result snippets (page fetching and Reddit were blocked, search budget ran out at ~11-30 searches per agent). Nothing here is validated. Treat as hypotheses to test with customer calls / pre-sells.

## Exit maths (report 10, partly inference)
- Small SaaS sells ~2.5-4x ARR (Acquire.com median ~3.9x profit).
- $10K MRR -> roughly $250-480K sale. $500K-1M needs roughly $17-33K MRR.

## Ranked shortlist (my synthesis)
1. Document/information chaser for small accounting & bookkeeping firms (r01 #2; echoed by r09 India CA firms / Brazil accountants). Sticky B2B recurring revenue, sellable. Medium confidence, best of the set.
2. Managed short-form clipping retainer for podcasters/founders at $500-1,500/mo (r04 #1). Fastest route to $10K MRR and uses existing clipping tools, but it is a service: sells for less than SaaS. Good cash bridge.
3. CBAM data kit for Indian MSME exporters (r05 #1). Hard EU deadline, India-based advantage; ARPU/one-off risk unproven.
4. Cross-vendor secrets/credential broker for coding agents (r07 #1). Loud GitHub demand (178+ thumbs-up) but no evidence of willingness to pay and vendor-absorption risk.
5. Home-inspection software wedge vs Spectora (r03 #1). Clear complaints, sticky vertical SaaS, but incumbents are funded.
6. Subscription/free-trial guardian reading inbox receipts (r06 #1). Measurable demand, consumer churn/crowding risk.
7. Exports compliance assistant for Indian freelancers (r01 #3), Brazil tax-reform readiness for accountants (r09 #1), QuickBooks/HoneyBook refugee tools (r01/r02 #1).

## Best fit for "$10K/month then sell for $500K-1M"
Accounting-firm document chaser (#1), sold to small firms (start with India CAs or US/UK bookkeepers), with the clipping retainer (#2) as a parallel cash bridge. Honest note: $10K MRR alone likely sells for ~$250-480K; plan for ~$20K+ MRR.

## Not verified
Willingness to pay for any idea; any Reddit/forum demand; most deadline dates against primary sources; sale comps (only 14 of 25 found); category-level exit speed.
