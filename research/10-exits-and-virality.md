## 2. Multiple analysis (aggregate data, all via search summaries)

Platform-wide data:
- **Acquire.com, Jan 2026 report:** the median confirmed SaaS profit multiple was 3.9x in both 2024 and 2025, with 129 deals reporting. Averages were in the low-to-mid 4x range. By size, net income under $100K averaged about 3.7x, and $100K-$1M about 3.9x. Source: https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/ and https://blog.acquire.com/acquisition-multiples-report-2025-findings-webinar-recap/
- **Acquire.com revenue guidance:** SaaS typically trades at 1x-3x annual revenue. Nearly all closed deals now come from profitable businesses. Source: https://blog.acquire.com/saas-valuation-multiples/
- **Asking vs. closing:** the BigIdeasDB set of 615 Acquire listings shows asks of about 2.6x revenue and 10.7x profit. Closed deals are much lower (3.9x profit). Treat asks as inflated. Source: https://bigideasdb.com/saas-valuation-multiples-2026
- **SaaS Capital:** bootstrapped private SaaS is predicted at 4.8x ARR and equity-backed at 5.3x. It covers $1M+ ARR companies, so it overstates what sub-$1M deals get. Source: https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/benchmarking-metrics-for-bootstrapped-saas-companies/
- **Broker tier table:** micro-SaaS ($50K-$1M ARR) at 2.5-4x ARR (4-6x SDE). Low reliability (broker marketing). Source: https://ctacquisitions.com/saas-business-valuation/

Rough multiple by MRR band. This is MY SYNTHESIS, not a measured table.

| Band (MRR) | Likely range | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Under $1K | Sells for $5K-$25K (asset-style) | Comps #4 and #6, thin evidence |
| $1K-$5K | ~1.5-3x ARR, or 3-4x profit | Acquire profit medians |
| $5K-$20K | ~2.5-4x ARR | Comps #2 and #14 |
| $20K+ | ~2-3x ARR for solo or founder-dependent businesses | Comp #1 |

Higher multiples appear in only a small fraction of deals, so do not plan around them.

**Implication for the founder's target.** A $500K-$1M sale at 2.5-4x ARR needs roughly $17K-$33K MRR, not $10K. At $10K MRR ($120K ARR), a realistic price is about $250K-$480K, before the 6-8% Acquire.com closing fee. The fee and $25-$100/mo listing cost come from https://bigideasdb.com/saas-valuation-multiples-2026 and are unverified. The $500K-$1M goal requires either higher margins/growth or $15K+ MRR. This is inference from the figures above.

What drives multiples, with evidence strength:
- **Profit.** Acquire says closers are almost all profitable, and it prices on profit. Strong evidence.
- **Platform risk.** Backmagic sold below 1x ARR, and the author blames Twitter API dependence (#3). Single anecdote.
- **Churn, customer concentration and paid-acquisition dependence.** Acquire's own comparison names high churn, one customer at 25% of revenue and heavy paid acquisition as the weak profile. No multiple quantification was found.
- **Growth.** A broker claims 30%+ ARR growth earns a premium over 10-15%. Weak, no numbers.
- **Seller owner-dependence and clean books.** Isora credits building in public and clean books (#5). Qualitative.
- **Pricing discipline.** One source says buyer interest drops to about 2% when a seller prices roughly 10% above fair value. I could not verify it.

## 4. Categories that exit best (weak evidence)

- Supported: **B2B SaaS with recurring workflow and high margin** (DialHawk call tracking, 89% margin; the paywall SaaS at 95%).
- Supported: **profitable over unprofitable.** Acquire says closed deals are nearly all profitable.
- Weak: **extensions and small tools** trade at low five figures unless revenue is proven (#4, #6).
- Not found: speed by category. I have no data on days-to-close by category. The only speed datapoints are RecordJoy at about 2 weeks, and Patterson's year-long process.

## 5. Playbook, $0 to $10K MRR to sale (inference, built from the above)

1. **Pick a B2B niche with a recurring workflow.** The two best-priced comps are B2B or niche-creator tools with 89-95% margins.
2. **Own your acquisition channel.** Use SEO, an extension, or integrations. Avoid dependence on a single platform API.
3. **Keep the books exit-ready from day one.** Use Stripe-connected metrics, separate expenses, and document processes. Isora credits this.
4. **Target $15K-$30K MRR, not $10K,** if the goal is $500K-$1M. See the arithmetic in section 2.
5. **Build in public** to raise buyer trust.
6. **List with a realistic price** based on profit, around 3.7-3.9x annual profit. Expect offers to close below asking.
7. **Budget time.** Patterson's process took a year. The small RecordJoy sale took weeks.
8. **Plan for fees.** Reported Acquire closing fee is 6-8% (unverified).
